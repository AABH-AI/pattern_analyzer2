  Caveat worth stating: a correlation over a queue's own history says a driver MOVES WITH
  demand, not that it CAUSED a particular week's miss. The engine uses these only to
  decide which drivers are worth citing for a given queue -- it retains a relationship
  per queue and rejects the rest, rather than assuming any driver applies everywhere.
