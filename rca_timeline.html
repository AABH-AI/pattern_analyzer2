✅ What already works

  • Open a weekly CSV/Excel in the browser — even 100,000+ rows.
  • Scan & flag every queue against the ±band.
  • Honest accuracy — the headline was a misleading ~99%; it now reads a truthful 77% on the full data.
  • Charts of volume and accuracy, sliceable across all 10 business dimensions.
  • Live database connection — pulls from SQL Server directly.

⏳ What's left before 30 July

  • Step 5 (RCA output) — the per-queue "why it missed" report + one-click digest of all flagged queues (main build).
  • Step 8 — validate with Prashant and set the tolerance; finish by 28 Jul to leave a spare day.
  • Step 9 — package and rehearse the demo.
  • The weekend (25–26 Jul) is kept light on purpose.

⚠️ What could slow us down

  • Access to the full real data — needed for steps 5 and 8; a delay squeezes the checking time.
  • The ±% tolerance (10% or 15%?) — needs a quick decision from Prashant.
  • The AI "explain in words" layer is a later project, not part of the 30 July demo.

🎬 What the 30 July demo will show

  • Pull the real data → the tool scans it → the off-forecast queues light up, with charts.
  • Click any flagged queue → a clear, data-backed reason it missed, plus the volumes.
  • One click → a summary of all flagged queues.
